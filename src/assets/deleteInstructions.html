Account Deletion Policy

Developer Name: Leuwint Technologies Pvt Ltd

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your account, follow these steps:

  • If you are a PD or a Chauffeur please contact administrator for account deletion.
  • Go to the 'Settings' section in our app.
  • Select 'Account Deletion'.
  • Confirm your request by clicking 'Delete My Account'.
  • Alternatively, you can email us at support@avalift.com with the subject 'Account Deletion Request'.

Data Deletion & Retention Policy

Upon account deletion, we handle your data as follows:

Deleted Data:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • App usage history

Retained Data:

  • Order or transaction details - retained for 5 years.
  • Legal compliance records (e.g., invoices, tax information) - retained for 5 years.
  • Customer support messages - retained for 60 months to assist with follow-ups.

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@avalift.com.
